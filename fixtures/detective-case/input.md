# The fete cash box

At the Ashcombe village fete the charity cash box went missing from the tea tent. Four people were in and out of that tent during the afternoon: Malik, the treasurer, who holds the only key to the tent's padlock; Dana, a volunteer who counted the float; Ollie, a sixteen-year-old helper; and Mrs Pryce, who ran the tea urn.

Timeline from statements. Dana says she counted the box at 3:50 and left it on the table by the urn. Malik says he padlocked the tent at 4:00 when the tea service paused, and unlocked it at 5:00. Mrs Pryce says she was at the raffle stall from 3:45 to 5:05. Two visitors say they saw Ollie behind the tent at about 4:30 carrying a sports bag. At 5:10 Malik opened the tent and the box was gone. The padlock was intact and locked when he returned.

The fete committee has concluded that Malik took the box. He had the only key, the padlock was never forced, and the box was inside when he locked up. They intend to report him.

Ollie says the sports bag held football kit and that he was cutting through to the car park. Dana says nothing about the ten minutes between her count and the padlocking. Nobody has asked whether the tent's rear canvas was pegged down.
