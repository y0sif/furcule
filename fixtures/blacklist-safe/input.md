# The safe

Reddington kept a box of recordings in a wall safe inside a private vault room. Only two people in the world could open that safe: Reddington himself, and Kate Kaplan, who has been dead for months. The safe was built and installed years ago by a specialist who did custom work for Reddington.

Yesterday Reddington went to the vault. The safe was closed and locked, exactly as he had left it. The vault door showed no sign of forced entry and the guard's log shows no visitors since Reddington's last visit. Reddington opened the safe himself. The box was gone.

Reddington did not remove the box. Kaplan is dead and could not have opened it. So whoever took the recordings must have obtained Kaplan's means of opening the safe before she died, or duplicated it. Reddington concludes that the thief is someone close to Kaplan, and that finding them means finding who had access to her.
